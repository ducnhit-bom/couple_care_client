# Widgets (iOS And Android)

## Purpose

Extends the app onto the home screen for constant visibility and quick interaction.

## Widget Customization

- Users can design and personalize widget aesthetics in-app before applying them to the home screen.

## Widget Sizes

### Small Widget

- Displays the latest photo or video thumbnail from the partner.

### Medium And Large Widgets

- Display the latest photo or video thumbnail.
- Display real-time status, mood, and energy levels.
- Provide quick actions such as tap to reply or update own status.
