# Introduction And Onboarding

## Purpose

Focuses on a frictionless entry and immediate connection between partners.

## Product Entry

- Splash screens and walkthroughs highlight the app's core values: private space, live widgets, and emotional tracking.

## Authentication Options

- Google account for quick login and signup.
- Apple account for quick login and signup.
- Phone number login with OTP verification.

## Partner Pairing

Pairing should feel flexible and intuitive.

- Code: enter a randomly generated alphanumeric code.
- QR code: scan directly from the partner's screen.
- Deeplink: open a shared link and confirm pairing in-app.
