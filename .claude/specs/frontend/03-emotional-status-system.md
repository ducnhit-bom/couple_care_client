# Emotional Status System

## Purpose

Defines the real-time emotional and availability signals shared between partners.

## Status Signals

- Status: current activity indicator such as busy, in a meeting, or sleeping.
- Mood: emotional indicators such as happy, tired, sad, stress, or custom moods.
- Energy: visual gauge from 0-100% that reflects physical or mental battery levels.
