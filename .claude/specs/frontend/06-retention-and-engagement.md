# Retention And Engagement

## Purpose

Features in this section build daily habits and help maintain relationship momentum.

## Smart Notifications

- Partner has low energy levels: prompts empathy and check-ins.
- Haven't interacted in a long time: triggers re-engagement.
- Big mood swings: alerts to sudden emotional shifts.

## Habit And Memory Features

- Streak: tracks consecutive days of interaction.
- Couple game: interactive mini-games designed for couples.
- Memory and moments: automated timeline and aggregation of shared media and significant events.
