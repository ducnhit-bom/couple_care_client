# Stories

## Purpose

Provides lightweight story sharing for couples, inspired by social media but scoped for two people.

## Photo Stories

- Upload from gallery or capture directly from camera.
- Add captions on the photo.
- Add background music.
- New photo stories should update the partner's home-screen widget automatically.
- New photo stories should trigger an immediate push notification to the partner.

## Video Stories

- Premium-only capability.
- Upload or record video stories.
- Includes captions, music, and push notifications to the partner.
