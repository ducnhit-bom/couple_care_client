# Subscription Model And Premium Access

## Purpose

Defines pricing tiers and the premium feature gates used across the product.

## Pricing Tiers

- Month: 9
- Year: 99
- Lifetime: 200 (75% sale off)

## Premium Feature Summary

- Archive memories in break mode.
- Block partner.
- Video stories.
- Extended text messages beyond 100 characters.
- Extended voice messages up to 60 seconds.
- Video calls.
