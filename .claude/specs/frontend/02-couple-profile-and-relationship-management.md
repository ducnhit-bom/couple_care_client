# Couple Profile And Relationship Management

## Purpose

A highly personalized space that reflects the identity of the relationship.

## Personalization

- Nickname partner: custom affectionate names.
- Anniversary date: automatically calculates relationship duration.
- Theme: app-wide color and UI customization.
- Avatar: individual or couple profile pictures.

## Conflict And Break Management

### Break

- Archive memories: safely lock and hide all shared data during a temporary break. Premium only.
- Delete everything: permanently erase all shared data and media.

### Block

- Temporarily sever interaction capabilities. Premium only.
