# Direct Messaging And Calls

## Purpose

Provides private couple communication with clear freemium limits that encourage subscription upgrades.

## Messaging

- Text messages are limited to 100 characters by default.
- Premium unlock extends text messaging beyond 100 characters.

## Voice Messages

- Voice recordings are limited to 10 seconds by default.
- Premium unlock extends voice recordings up to 60 seconds.

## Video Calls

- Optional high-quality face-to-face communication.
- Premium unlock required.
