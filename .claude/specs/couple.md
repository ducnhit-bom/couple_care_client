# FEATURE SPECIFICATION DOCUMENT: COUPLES CONNECTION APP

**Platform:** Mobile App (Flutter) & Native Widgets (iOS/Android).

**Product Vision:** To create a private, deeply engaging space exclusively for couples, featuring real-time emotional tracking, seamless memory sharing, and home-screen connectivity.

## 1. INTRODUCTION & ONBOARDING

Focuses on a frictionless entry and immediate connection between partners.

**Introduce:** Splash screens and walkthroughs highlighting core values (private space, live widgets, emotional tracking).

**Onboarding:**

**Google Account:** Quick login/signup.

**Apple Account:** Quick login/signup.

**Phone Number:** Traditional login with OTP verification.

**Pairing:** Flexible and intuitive connection flows.

**Code:** Enter a randomly generated alphanumeric code.

**QR Code:** Scan a QR code directly from the partner's screen.

**Deeplink:** Click a shared link to automatically open the app and confirm the pairing.

## 2. COUPLE PROFILE & RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT

A highly personalized space to reflect the unique identity of the relationship.

**Personalization:**

**Nickname Partner:** Custom affectionate names.

**Anniversary Date:** Automatically calculates the duration of the relationship.

**Theme:** App-wide color and UI customization.

**Avatar:** Individual or couple profile pictures.

**Conflict & Break Management:**

**Break:**

*Archive memories:* Safely lock and hide all data during a temporary break (**Subscribe to unlock**).

*Delete everything:* Permanently erase all shared data and media.

**Block:** Temporarily sever interaction capabilities (**Subscribe to unlock**).

## 3. EMOTIONAL STATUS SYSTEM

**Status:** Current activity indicator (e.g., busy, in a meeting, sleeping).

**Mood:** Emotional indicators such as happy, tired, sad, stress, or custom moods.

**Energy:** A visual gauge (0-100%) reflecting physical or mental battery levels.

## 4. STORIES & COMMUNICATION

Simulating popular social media features, but exclusively scaled down for two people.

### 4.1. Stories

**Photo:**

Upload from the gallery or capture directly via the camera.

Add captions on the photo.

Add background music.

*Key Features:* Photos automatically update to the partner's home screen widget and trigger an immediate push notification upon upload.

**Video (Subscribe to unlock):**

Premium capability to upload or record video stories.

Includes captions, music, and push notifications to the partner.

### 4.2. Direct Messaging & Calls

Implementing a strategic freemium model to drive subscriptions.

**Message:** Default limit of 100 characters per text. (**Unlock to text more**).

**Voice:** Default limit of 10 seconds per recording. (**Unlock for longer voice recording - max 60s**).

**Video Call (Optional):** High-quality face-to-face communication. (**Unlock to use**).

## 5. RETENTION & ENGAGEMENT

Features designed to build daily habits and maintain relationship momentum.

**Smart Notifications:**

*Partner has low energy levels:* Prompts empathy and check-ins.

*Haven't interacted in a long time:* Re-engagement trigger.

*Big mood swings:* Alerts to sudden emotional shifts.

**Streak:** Tracks consecutive days of interaction to encourage daily app usage.

**Couple Game:** Interactive mini-games designed for couples.

**Memory & Moments:** Automated timeline and aggregation of shared media and significant events.

## 6. WIDGETS (iOS & Android)

Extending the app's presence directly to the user's home screen for constant visibility.

**Custom Widget:** In-app tools to design and personalize widget aesthetics before applying them to the home screen.

**Small Widget:**

Displays the latest photo or video thumbnail from the partner.

**Medium/Large Widget:**

Displays the latest photo or video thumbnail.

Displays real-time Status, Mood, and Energy levels.

Provides quick actions (e.g., tap to reply, update own status).

## 7. SUBSCRIPTION MODEL (MONETIZATION)

Tiers designed to offer flexibility while locking premium communication and data preservation tools behind a paywall.

**Tiers:**

Month: 9

Year: 99

Lifetime: 200 (75% sale off)

**Premium Feature Summary:** Archive memories (Break mode), Block partner, Video Stories, Extended text messages (>100 chars), Extended voice messages (up to 60s), Video calls.
